Common Questions

Things clients usually ask.

What types of clients do you work with?
We work with government bodies (municipalities, commissioners), educational institutions, and enterprises. Our biggest strength is public-sector analytics — we're currently engaged with Coimbatore Corporation.
How long does a typical project take?
Timelines depend on scope. A website is typically 2–4 weeks. A full analytics pipeline with Power BI dashboards takes 4–8 weeks. Government data projects are scoped individually. We always share a clear timeline before starting.
Do you provide ongoing support after delivery?
Yes — all deliverables include documentation and a handover session. We also offer ongoing support contracts for dashboards, websites, and portals that need regular updates or maintenance.
What formats do your reports and dashboards come in?
We deliver Power BI interactive dashboards, PDF summary reports, Excel workbooks, and web-based dashboards depending on client needs. Government deliverables are formatted to official reporting standards.
Can you work with our existing data systems?
Yes. We integrate with MySQL, Excel, CSV, Google Sheets, and most SQL-based databases. We also handle data cleansing and restructuring when working with messy legacy data sources.
